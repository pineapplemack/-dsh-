02 / Notes on practiceEst. 2018 · Shanghai

A manifesto, lightly held.

“I make images that feel like memories of places you haven’t been yet.”

✳

Half studio, half field notes. I move between art direction, photography, type and code — usually all in the same week. My work is warm, a little off-centre, and built to be lived with rather than scrolled past.

Before MACKOWN, I spent five years inside magazines and two inside a very small film company. I learned that the best ideas tend to arrive after the third coffee and before the second draft.
